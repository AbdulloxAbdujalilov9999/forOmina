Love is not something to end...
The love? Love is not something to end, love is not something you care about that person. Love is not being perfect, love is not something you can have willingly. It comes unintentionally, a person can not love someone just by saying you love them. Becoz Love is not action of mind or brain. It is act of soul. You love someone only if Allah wants you to, you love someone unintentionally, unconditionally, not just becoz they are beautiful, not only becoz you have memories with them. Not becoz of anything. Only then you can realize how much you need that certain person, only then you can see how much important that person is. In darkness... with none.. and with nothing...
