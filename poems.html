Hechkim bilolmaydi holimni meni... Chunki eng sodiq oshig'ing menman... Chunki men uchun eng go'zal sensan... Izladim tubini ko'zing dengizin Ahr bunchalar ham manoli ko'zlar Har narsa isbotlar, o'zing tengsizim Mayin, maftunkor va mazmunli so'zlar... The love?
Love is not something to end...
The love? Love is not something to end, love is not something you care about that person. Love is not being perfect, love is not something you can have willingly. It comes unintentionally, a person can not love someone just by saying you love them. Becoz Love is not action of mind or brain. It is act of soul. You love someone only if Allah wants you to, you love someone unintentionally, unconditionally, not just becoz they are beautiful, not only becoz you have memories with them. Not becoz of anything. If you know why you love someone, then that love wont last long, becoz it is worldly, and everything in this world will vanish eventually. And love... it comes with price, with burden and with pain... And sometimes the pain is not something you expect to be easy. Becoz it is hard to keep, hard to save. And when I say loving is not easy, I mean it. You realize you love someone mostly when you are in darkness, when you had the times so rough you start to will death. Only then you can realize how much you need that certain person, only then you can see how much important that person is. In darkness... with none.. and with nothing... At time like this, you only want your loved one to be there, to hold, to lean on...
